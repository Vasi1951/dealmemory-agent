# DealMemory selection-round demo

Run `streamlit run app.py`. The walkthrough takes approximately 2–3 minutes and works in Demo Mode without API keys.

## 1. Establish the product

Open **Deals**.

Say: “Most sales AI can answer a question. DealMemory remembers what happened to us.”

Point to the deal table and the compact `Memory` control in the top bar. The product has three views: Deals, Insights, and Memory.

## 2. Show the experience layer

Open **Memory** and point to:

- D-097: pricing resistance, early discounting, `LOST`.
- D-104: similar pricing resistance, ROI-first positioning and technical validation, `WON`.

Say: “The important asset is not a transcript. It is the organization’s accumulated experience.”

## 3. Create the hero moment

Return to **Deals** and click **+ Analyze New Deal**.

Use the pre-filled scenario:

- Logistics
- `$240,000`
- Evaluation
- Competitor X
- Pricing Objection
- Integration concern in the customer context

Leave `Memory` ON and click **ANALYZE DEAL**.

## 4. Read the brief

In the deal workspace, show:

1. **AI Deal Brief:** lead with quantified ROI before commercial concessions, then use technical proof.
2. **Evidence:** D-097 and D-104 are shown as historical experiences with relevance and match reasons.
3. **Knowledge:** Vault fallback references value selling and technical proof.
4. **Why this recommendation?** The concise reasoning trail connects the current pricing signal to the two different historical outcomes.

Say: “This is not generic sales advice. The recommendation is a response to the contrast between what worked and what failed.”

## 5. Close the loop

In **Record outcome**, select `WON`, add a short note such as `ROI proof converted price resistance`, and click **Save outcome**.

Say: “That outcome is now part of organizational memory.”

Analyze another similar deal. The new `NEW-001` experience appears in future retrieval. Open **Insights** to show that the metrics and patterns are derived from the dataset, not decorative charts.

## Optional comparison

Turn `Memory` OFF and analyze the same deal again. Historical evidence disappears and the brief explicitly says it is a generic recommendation based on current context and general knowledge.

Close with: “The system does not just retrieve information. It accumulates experience.”
